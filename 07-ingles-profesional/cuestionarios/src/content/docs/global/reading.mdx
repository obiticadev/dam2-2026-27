**15 preguntas · nivel medio · cuatro opciones · una correcta.** Tres lecturas integradas: prácticas profesionales, un club de lectura y un proyecto vecinal.

Repaso de los dos exámenes: los cuatro tiempos del presente, vocabulario combinado y lectura integrada. Son preguntas nuevas, no una copia de las páginas por examen; el reparto es pedagógico, no una ponderación oficial.

[Consultar la guía antes de empezar](/guia-estudio/) o resolver primero y repasar después de corregir. En Reading, cada pregunta conserva su texto para poder barajarlas sin perder contexto.

**Reading · An internship journal** (texto original de práctica)

> I usually start my internship at nine and spend the first half hour checking support requests. This week I am working with the testing team instead. I have been checking a new booking system since Monday, and I have reported seven errors so far.
>
> Yesterday my supervisor asked me to explain one of those errors in a meeting. I felt nervous, but the team listened carefully and asked useful questions. They have already fixed that error, although they have not checked all my other reports yet. Tomorrow I am meeting the designer to discuss a confusing button. The appointment is in my calendar. I still help with urgent support requests when necessary, so the temporary testing work has not replaced all my normal duties.

What is different about the writer’s work this week?

- [x] They are spending time with the testing team.
  > Correcta: This week presenta el cambio respecto a revisar peticiones de soporte al empezar.
- [ ] They are starting work three hours earlier.
  > No se indica un nuevo horario de entrada.
- [ ] They have permanently stopped all support work.
  > La última frase mantiene las peticiones urgentes como parte de sus tareas.
- [ ] They are working as the company’s designer.
  > Va a reunirse con el diseñador, no a sustituirlo.

Examen 1: present simple y present continuous. Frecuencia, rutinas y situaciones actuales. Vocabulario seleccionado como apoyo en la bóveda; Reading es práctica añadida, no una lectura oficial asignada al examen.

[Consultar la guía antes de empezar](/guia-estudio/) o resolver primero y repasar después de corregir. En Reading, cada pregunta conserva su texto para poder barajarlas sin perder contexto.

<Quiz id="ing-e1-r-01" title="Help desk · Main idea">
**Reading · A different week at the help desk** (texto original de práctica)

> Nora works at a college help desk. She usually checks emails before the first students arrive, and she takes a short break at eleven. She does not normally work outside the office.
>
> This week, however, the college is installing new computers in the library. Nora is helping students there every morning while her colleague answers calls at the help desk. The library is quieter than the office, but the students need more help with the new login system. Nora is writing a short guide because she wants students to solve simple problems without waiting for a technician. On Friday she is meeting the librarian at two; they already have an appointment. Next week she returns to her normal office routine.

What is the main purpose of the text?

- [x] To describe a temporary change in Nora’s work.
  > Correcta: La rutina normal contrasta con “This week, however” y la vuelta a la oficina la semana siguiente.
- [ ] To explain why Nora is leaving her job.
  > No se menciona dimisión; “Next week she returns” indica continuidad.
- [ ] To compare salaries at a college.
  > No aparecen sueldos ni una comparación económica.
- [ ] To explain how to install a computer.
  > Se menciona la instalación, pero no se dan instrucciones técnicas.

> Leo wakes up at six forty-five but stays in bed for another fifteen minutes. He gets dressed, has breakfast and leaves home at seven thirty. He normally cycles to the training centre because he enjoys the exercise. On rainy days he takes the bus instead.

He usually arrives fifteen minutes before they begin, which gives him time to talk to his classmates.

At what time does Leo get out of bed?

- [ ] At six forty-five.
  > Es la hora de wake up, no de levantarse de la cama.
- [ ] At seven thirty.
  > Es la hora habitual de salir de casa.
- [ ] At eight thirty.
  > Es la hora de comienzo de las clases.
- [x] At seven o’clock.
  > Correcta: Se despierta a las 6:45 y permanece quince minutos más: get up es a las 7:00.

**Reading · A quiet hour for the team** (texto original de práctica)

> At a small software company, the developers start each day with a short meeting at nine. They explain their plans and ask for help if necessary. After the meeting, they normally keep the next hour free from calls so they can concentrate on difficult tasks.

They believe these reviews help everyone learn; they are not a competition to find the worst developer.

Why does the team normally avoid calls during the hour after the meeting?

- [ ] To avoid speaking to new colleagues.
  > Sam está ayudando precisamente a un compañero nuevo.
- [ ] To wait for lunch without doing anything.
  > La hora se reserva para trabajar concentrados, no para esperar sin hacer nada.
- [x] To focus on demanding work.
  > Correcta: “Concentrate on difficult tasks” explica la hora sin llamadas.
- [ ] To finish work for the day.
  > La jornada acaba de empezar; no se dice que terminen a esa hora.
